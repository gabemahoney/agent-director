# A per-spawn idle_screen override is refused out of range, recorded, and wins over the configuration across calls

Maps to Epic t1.sp6.rk acceptance 2 and Epic t1.sp6.6s acceptance 7 and 10:
`--idle-screen-timeout-seconds` refusals before any write or launch, the
recorded override, its use by later independent reads in place of
`[state] idle_screen_timeout_seconds`, and one
`ad.spawn.displayed_state_transition` record for the one entry.

## Setup

The DB-reset fixture gives a fresh store at the binary's current schema. The
driver writes `[state] settle_ms = 0`, `idle_screen_timeout_seconds = 60`,
`needs_intelligence_timeout_seconds = 30` and the Codex stand-in as
`[launch] codex_path`, and records the case start time. All SQL uses
`/opt/driver/sql.sh`.

## Steps

1. Spawn the stand-in Claude with `--idle-screen-timeout-seconds` `-1`,
   `1.5`, `x` and `9223372037` in turn, each with its own instance id.
2. Spawn the stand-in Claude as `override` with
   `--idle-screen-timeout-seconds 7200`, type its `SessionStart` and
   `UserPromptSubmit`, and commit its first baseline (as in
   t2.sp6.pane.public step 2).
3. Move the baseline's start 1 hour back; run `status`.
4. Move it 2 more hours back; run `status` until it commits (exactly 1), then
   `get` in a fresh process.
5. Read the trail's records for `override`.

## Pass criteria

- Step 1: every spawn exits non-zero with `err_name` `ErrInvalidFlags` on
  stderr and leaves no row for its id.
- Step 2: `spawns.idle_screen_timeout_seconds` is 7200.
- Step 3: `status` shows `working` with exactly the keys `context`,
  `family_state`, `idle_screen_availability` and `state`, although an hour
  unchanged exceeds the configured 60 s.
- Step 4: `status` (exactly the idle_screen keys of t2.sp6.pane.public) and
  `get` (no `stuck_reason` member) show state `working` and
  `displayed_state` `idle_screen` with
  `unchanged_minutes` 180, availability `available`, the attach target
  `cd-override` and a `since` made during the case; the override is still
  7200.
- Step 5: exactly one `ad.spawn.displayed_state_transition` record, with
  exactly the keys `event`, `instance_id`, `new_state`, `prior_state` and
  `ts`: `working` to `idle_screen`, `ts` equal to step 4's `since`. Step 3's
  read wrote none.
- The trail holds no `ad.spawn.stuck_transition` record, and no
  `ad.spawn.state_transition` record names `idle_screen` or
  `needs_intelligence`.
