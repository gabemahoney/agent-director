## Setup

Use the exact-revision shipped CLI, shared driver/runtime and private Linux namespace. The tmux wrapper pauses only fixture evidence queries after independent committed acknowledgement; it does not replace or fake production authority. SQL and faults all use /opt/driver/sql.sh. Cleanup releases the case's gates and affects only owned helpers and the private socket.

## Steps
