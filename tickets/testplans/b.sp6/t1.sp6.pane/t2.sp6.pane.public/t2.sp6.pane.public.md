# Claude and Codex expose a sampled unchanged pane as no_screen_change through independent reads

Maps to Epic t1.sp6.rk acceptance 1 and 3: the first committed capture starts
the baseline, a read at the timeout enters `stuck` / `no_screen_change`, and
every surface shows the same committed detail (PRD t1.ob8.s4).

## Setup

The DB-reset fixture gives a fresh store at the binary's current schema. The
driver writes `[state] settle_ms = 0`, `no_screen_change_timeout_seconds =
1200` and the Codex stand-in as `[launch] codex_path`, and records the case
start time. All SQL uses `/opt/driver/sql.sh`.

## Steps

1. For each of `claude` and `codex`, spawn the stand-in as `pane-<agent>`
   (session `cd-pane-<agent>`) and type its `SessionStart` and
   `UserPromptSubmit` into its pane; wait until the raw state is `working`.
2. Run `status` until it commits an observation (`spawns.stuck_version`
   moves by exactly 1, at most 5 reads); it must show `working`, and the
   committed `pane_sha256` must equal `read-pane`'s default `pane_sha256`
   (re-read at most 5 times while the stand-in's prompt is still drawing).
3. Move the baseline's start 2 hours back with `sql.sh`.
4. Run `status` until it commits (exactly 1), then `get` in a fresh process,
   then `list --state stuck`.

## Pass criteria

- Steps 4's `status` and `get` show the fixture's `state` and `stuck_reason`,
  `unchanged_minutes` 120, `tmux_session_name` `cd-pane-<agent>`,
  `no_screen_change_availability` `available`, no `request_token` or
  `tool_name`, and the same `since` in exact millisecond UTC no earlier than
  the case start (the committing read, not the backdated baseline).
- `get` keeps its `permission_requests` and `unproven_requests` arrays.
- `list` holds the row with `stuck_reason` `no_screen_change` and
  availability `available`, and no `since` or `unchanged_minutes`; it commits
  nothing more (`stuck_version` unchanged), and the raw state is still
  `working`.
- The evidence line shows both agents' minutes and each entry's
  `stuck_version` delta (`entry_stuck_version_delta`, 1 for each agent).
