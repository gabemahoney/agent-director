spec/tla/codex/ provenance

Scope of historical results and current files
  The b.ssz results in README.md belong to its 41 submitted model snapshots and
  configurations. b.w21 subsequently changed src/B146R5Cx.tla with
  default-off legacy scenarios and repair knobs. The historical final-model
  equivalence results do not validate this evolving source. Every pin
  identifies current file bytes and claims no TLC result.

What this is
  Bee b.ssz's TLA+ check of the Codex design changes: the Codex session-end
  rule (SessionEnd as an end report, `ended` by find-missing and pause) and
  the Codex state mapping and permission relay (Interrupt as a turn end, no
  tool_ran proof, no PostToolUse close, no idle-prompt Notification). Three
  models carry the knobs: Phase5HookCx.tla (the hook gate and the row's life),
  Hold36hCx.tla (the working hold and the relay's proofs) and B146R5Cx.tla
  (the integrated relay model). Every knob is FALSE by default, so each cfg
  that leaves them off checks its source model unchanged. With the models
  come their 41 cfgs and the job table. Bee
  b.ssz owns the models and their results. The
  files are vendored as the record of the model checking done before the
  product code of tasks E4-T3 (find-missing and pause on Codex rows) and
  E4-T4 (permission relay on Codex rows).

E2 authoring overlay (b.jf1, 2026-10-14; NOT MODEL CHECKED)
  src/Hold36hCx.tla now separates Eligible(r) from ChildID(r).
  CxUnknownOrigin represents an ineligible request whose child identity is
  NULL. CxIdentityProof is a negative control that infers proof from NULL.
  src/cfg/rx_U_null_safe.cfg expects KeysSafe PASS; rx_U_null_control.cfg
  expects a KeysSafe violation. Both are authored selections only, outside
  the historical suite.tsv and job receipts. Actual TLC requires external
  scheduler execution of the changed source at the final commit.
  The 41 historical results in README.md apply to the model copies their jobs
  submitted, never this E2 overlay.

Not wired into make tla
  No model-execution target runs these files. `make tla` runs ci/suite.tsv only,
  and ci/run_ci.sh never reads codex/. To run a job again, build its
  job-<run>/ directory (Dockerfile, spec/tla/jobs/lib/tlcjob.sh, the model,
  its cfg, runs and spec/tla/tla2tools.jar) and submit it to the job
  scheduler.

Source
  Bee b.ssz, directory tickets/Ideas/b.ssz/tla/ of the ticket tree
  (outside this repo). Copied byte for byte with rsync -a on 2026-10-12,
  after the last result came in, and checked equal to the source with diff
  -r. The 41 job directories are no longer vendored. Every job's jar was
  checked byte-identical (sha256sum) to spec/tla/tla2tools.jar, SHA-256
  32d64fbbc464559fc7192341b27b885fa4eb6b92d1648d2b49fb9cdcb7aacf81, and its
  tlcjob.sh to spec/tla/jobs/lib/tlcjob.sh. Their cfgs equal
  src/cfg/<run>.cfg.
  Every historical Hold36hCx job copy predates the E2 live-src overlay;
  none validates it.
