# Claude and Codex show a sampled unchanged pane as idle_screen at the configured timeout through independent reads

Maps to Epic t1.sp6.rk acceptance 1 and 3 and Epic t1.sp6.6s acceptance 2, 6
and 10: the first committed capture starts the baseline, a read past the
configured `[state] idle_screen_timeout_seconds` enters `idle_screen`, every
surface shows the same committed detail, and the entry writes one
`ad.spawn.displayed_state_transition` record (PRD t1.ob8.s4).

## Setup

The DB-reset fixture gives a fresh store at the binary's current schema. The
driver writes `[state] settle_ms = 0`, `idle_screen_timeout_seconds = 600`
(not the 1200 default), `needs_intelligence_timeout_seconds = 30` and the
Codex stand-in as `[launch] codex_path`, and records the case start time. All
SQL uses `/opt/driver/sql.sh`.

## Steps

1. For each of `claude` and `codex`, spawn the stand-in as `pane-<agent>`
   (session `cd-pane-<agent>`) and type its `SessionStart` and
   `UserPromptSubmit` into its pane; wait until the raw state is `working`.
2. Run `status` until it commits an observation (`spawns.observation_version`
   moves by exactly 1, at most 5 reads); it must show `working`, and the
   committed `pane_sha256` must equal `read-pane`'s default `pane_sha256`
   (re-read at most 5 times while the stand-in's prompt is still drawing).
3. Move the baseline's start 15 minutes (900 s) back with `sql.sh`: 300 s past
   the configured 600 and 300 s short of the 1200 default, so only a binary
   that reads the key shows the state. Job latency only adds elapsed time.
4. Run `status` until it commits (exactly 1), then `get` in a fresh process,
   then `list --state idle_screen` and `list --state working`.
5. Read the trail's records for `pane-<agent>`.

## Pass criteria

- Step 4's `status` shows exactly the keys `context`, `displayed_state`,
  `family_state`, `idle_screen_availability`, `since`, `state`,
  `tmux_session_name` and `unchanged_minutes`: the fixture's `state`
  (`working`) and `displayed_state` (`idle_screen`), `unchanged_minutes` 15,
  `tmux_session_name` `cd-pane-<agent>` and `idle_screen_availability`
  `available`.
- `get` shows the same state, displayed state, minutes, attach target and availability, no
  `request_token` or `tool_name`, no `stuck_reason` member, and keeps its
  `permission_requests` and `unproven_requests` arrays.
- Both show the same `since` in exact millisecond UTC no earlier than the case
  start (the committing read, not the backdated baseline).
- `list --state idle_screen` and `list --state working` (b.zpv: the real
  state still matches) each hold the row once with state `working`,
  `displayed_state` `idle_screen`, availability `available` and the list
  shape of docs/caller-contract.md (`displayed_state`, no `since` or detail);
  they commit nothing more (`observation_version` unchanged), and the raw
  state is still `working`.
- The trail holds exactly one `ad.spawn.displayed_state_transition` record for
  the row, with exactly the keys `event`, `instance_id`, `new_state`,
  `prior_state` and `ts`: `working` to `idle_screen`, `ts` equal to the shown
  `since`.
- The trail holds no `ad.spawn.stuck_transition` record, and no
  `ad.spawn.state_transition` record names `idle_screen` or
  `needs_intelligence`.
- The evidence line shows the timeout 600, both agents' minutes, each entry's
  `observation_version` delta (`entry_commits`, 1 for each agent) and their
  record counts.
