# Test case: Three launches and detached useful worker after stop

Maps Task 3 AC1, PRD AC12 and SR-7.1/SR-9.

## Setup

Use the existing offline synthetic ACP and public CLI driver, one private HOME,
store, cwd and tmux server. A/B share explicit config/data; C selects distinct
directories. Each gets a unique runtime script, evidence files and tmux name.
A starts the recognized same-runtime `index.js worker-server` without ACP pipes.

## Steps

1. Complete one correlated operation per launch and observe all three live bindings.
2. Perform a bounded worker operation; retain its PID/starttime and received selectors.
3. Hold a real B permission. Have A's detached worker invoke the actual hook CLI
   with B's current claim; require the complete B row/binding/request/history snapshot
   unchanged. Allow B's own request and observe its synthetic tool result/completion.
4. Publicly pause A, confirm exact gateway/runtime death and no protocol retry.
5. Complete new B/C work with unchanged bindings and selected artifacts, then perform
   different worker work with unchanged identity. Rotate B through public `/new`,
   replay the retained obsolete claim from the worker, require unchanged authority,
   and complete another legitimate current B operation.
6. End peers and release the exact worker only after survival observations. Retain
   per-launch observed JSON, traces, selectors, bindings and authority snapshots.

## Pass criteria

Exact correlated results and artifact inputs/PIDs/cwd/selections; simultaneous
launches; A stop leaves B/C and the useful worker operational with same identities.
Foreign/stale hook attempts leave the target/request unchanged; exact final method
counts expose retries/fallback. Static parsing is author evidence only; scheduler
execution remains pending. Synthetic AD authority and directory selection do not
prove credentials, native cross-account workers, policy repair, renewal or macOS.
The separate verified-rotation unit owner exercises stale permission SQL/proof guards.
