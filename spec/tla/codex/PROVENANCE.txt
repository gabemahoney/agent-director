Scope of historical results and current files
  The b.ssz results in README.md belong to its 41 submitted model snapshots and
  configurations. b.w21 subsequently changed src/B146R5Cx.tla with
  default-off legacy scenarios and repair knobs. b.y7g later extended
  src/Phase5HookCx.tla with busy-pause signaling and added its focused suite.
  The historical final-model
  equivalence results do not validate this evolving source. Every pin
  identifies current file bytes and claims no TLC result.

What this is
  Bee b.ssz's TLA+ check of the Codex design changes: the Codex session-end
  rule (SessionEnd as an end report, `ended` by find-missing and pause) and
  the Codex state mapping and permission relay (Interrupt as a turn end, no
  tool_ran proof, no PostToolUse close, no idle-prompt Notification). Three
  models carry the knobs: Phase5HookCx.tla (the hook gate and the row's life),
  Hold36hCx.tla (the working hold and the relay's proofs) and B146R5Cx.tla
  (the integrated relay model). Every knob is FALSE by default, so each cfg
  that leaves them off checks its source model unchanged. With the models
  come their 41 cfgs and the job table. Bee
  b.ssz owns the models and their results. The
  files are vendored as the record of the model checking done before the
  product code of tasks E4-T3 (find-missing and pause on Codex rows) and
  E4-T4 (permission relay on Codex rows).

E2 authoring overlay (b.jf1, 2026-10-14; NOT MODEL CHECKED)
  src/Hold36hCx.tla now separates Eligible(r) from ChildID(r).
  CxUnknownOrigin represents an ineligible request whose child identity is
  NULL. CxIdentityProof is a negative control that infers proof from NULL.
  src/cfg/rx_U_null_safe.cfg expects KeysSafe PASS; rx_U_null_control.cfg
  expects a KeysSafe violation. Both are authored selections only, outside
  the historical suite.tsv and job receipts. Actual TLC requires external
  scheduler execution of the changed source at the final commit.
  The 41 historical results in README.md apply to the model copies their jobs
  submitted, never this E2 overlay.

Busy-pause overlay (b.y7g, 2026-11-21; scheduler checked)
  src/Phase5HookCx.tla adds default-off BusyPauseOn and PauseSignalOn.
  Busy Codex pause captures launch/PID/start identity, sends no keys, and
  permits process Exit without SessionEnd. Its wait writes ended for the
  same launch, including process-based recovery marking missing during the
  pause (covered parity, b.nqq); initial terminal rows are a no-op and a
  changed launch is left alone. Existing properties remain, with the stronger
  PauseSignalEndsSameLaunch property added to cfg/ps_live.cfg.
  pause-signal-suite.tsv is a current runner-format manifest with nine
  cfg/ps_*.cfg selections: liveness, unchanged legacy pause, two safety actor
  families, signal-off and PID-only negative controls, and reachability probes.
  These results belong to Phase5HookCx.tla SHA-256
  7e4ab389e8bda66490c894ecb6880151bbe33e17801801bc51b9f4a058ed4efe:
    preflight     j20261121071358-2db853  PASS (SANY/import and evaluation)
    signal-live   j20261121071425-cea78a  PASS (2/2 expected outcomes)
    signal-red    j20261121071951-7b6ae4  PASS (1/1 expected violation)
    signal-safe   j20261121072018-de1be9  PASS (3/3 expected outcomes)
    signal-probes j20261121072448-4bac00  PASS (3/3 expected violations)
  The complete run matched all nine expectations. README.md documents bounds,
  fairness and the finite-instance scope. It does not validate the other
  models or their overlays, nor does it validate the product implementation.

Startup-state overlay (b.hib, 2026-11-25; focused checks in progress)
  src/Hold36hCx.tla and src/B146R5Cx.tla add one finite delayed SessionStart
  behind default-off HibRelax. The hib_*.cfg companions and src/hib-suite.tsv
  retain original bounds and properties, with active-state/idle preservation
  assertions and explicit negative controls. Current pins identify source
  bytes only; partial scheduler results do not validate the complete suite.

Running the focused suite
  Default make tla still selects ci/suite.tsv. Override TLA_SUITE with
  spec/tla/codex/pause-signal-suite.tsv and TLA_SPEC_DIR with
  spec/tla/codex/src, using make -j1 tla and the scheduler settings in README.md.
  The historical codex/suite.tsv remains an inventory in its old format;
  it is not a runner-format manifest.
