What this is
  Bee b.zuj's TLA+ model of the Phase 1 LABEL design and its on-demand
  model-check suite, vendored so `make tla` can submit it to the job
  scheduler (Epic 22, t1.h98.8p; subtask t3.h98.8p.q1.74). Bee b.zuj owns
  the model and its results: nothing in this tree changes the specs, the
  cfgs, the manifest or the expectations. To re-sync, copy the files from
  b.zuj again and replace the pin lines at the end of this file.

  this repo). Copied byte for byte with cp on 2026-10-02. Every file's SHA-256
  is in the pin list below; each was checked equal to its b.zuj source with
  sha256sum at copy time.

  default of `make tla`); full 92 runs (every row; optional).
  Specs: Phase4 5 rows, Phase4Split 65 rows, Phase5Hook 22 rows.
