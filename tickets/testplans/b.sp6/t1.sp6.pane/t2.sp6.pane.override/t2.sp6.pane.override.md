# A per-spawn no_screen_change override is refused out of range, recorded, and wins over the configuration across calls

Maps to Epic t1.sp6.rk acceptance 2: `--no-screen-change-timeout-seconds`
refusals before any write or launch, the recorded override, and its use by
later independent reads in place of `[state] no_screen_change_timeout_seconds`.

## Setup

The DB-reset fixture gives a fresh store at the binary's current schema. The
driver writes `[state] settle_ms = 0`, `no_screen_change_timeout_seconds = 60`
and the Codex stand-in as `[launch] codex_path`. All SQL uses
`/opt/driver/sql.sh`.

## Steps

1. Spawn the stand-in Claude with `--no-screen-change-timeout-seconds` `-1`,
   `1.5`, `x` and `9223372037` in turn, each with its own instance id.
2. Spawn the stand-in Claude as `override` with
   `--no-screen-change-timeout-seconds 7200`, type its `SessionStart` and
   `UserPromptSubmit`, and commit its first baseline (as in
   t2.sp6.pane.public step 2).
3. Move the baseline's start 1 hour back; run `status`.
4. Move it 2 more hours back; run `status` until it commits (exactly 1), then
   `get` in a fresh process.

## Pass criteria

- Step 1: every spawn exits non-zero with `err_name` `ErrInvalidFlags` on
  stderr and leaves no row for its id.
- Step 2: `spawns.no_screen_change_timeout_seconds` is 7200.
- Step 3: `status` shows `working` with no `stuck_reason`, although an hour
  unchanged exceeds the configured 60 s.
- Step 4: `status` and `get` show `no_screen_change` with `unchanged_minutes`
  180, the attach target `cd-override` and a `since` made during the case;
  the override is still 7200.
